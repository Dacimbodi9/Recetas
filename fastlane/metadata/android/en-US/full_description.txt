Sazón is your personal cookbook — beautifully crafted, privacy-first, and offline.

🍳 YOUR KITCHEN, YOUR CRAFT

Over 1,000 built-in recipes. Create your own. Plan your week.
All offline. No accounts. No tracking. Ever.

✨ Features

*  **Offline & Private:** All your data stays on your device. No tracking, no servers.
*  **Smart Search:** Fast fuzzy search by name, or select ingredients you have in your fridge to find matching recipes.
*  **Dietary Compliance:** Permanent dietary filters (Vegan, Gluten-Free, Keto, etc.) with incompatible recipe warnings.
*  **Meal Planner:** Weekly calendar with reusable templates.
*  **Shopping List:** Auto-generated from your meal plan.
*  **Nutrition Stats:** Calories, macros, BMI, consistency streaks.
*  **Organization:** Create folders, rate your recipes, and manage your favorites.
*  **Premium Experience:** Artisan interface with beautiful dark mode.
